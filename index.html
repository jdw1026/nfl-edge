Sources: Polymarket Gamma API (game + player-prop events, best bid/ask), ESPN scoreboard (DraftKings spread/ML/total with juice) and ESPN injury report. Spread and total fair values come from a key-number-aware NFL margin model fitted to the book's line and moneyline; moneyline fair values are the de-vigged book price. Ruled-out players rely on Polymarket's rule that an inactive player's market resolves "No"/"Under".
